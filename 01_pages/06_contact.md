# 06 — Contact

A pared-down, executive-grade contact page. No noisy form. No newsletter pitch. The page exists to make starting a conversation easy and fast for the small number of people Yoann actually wants to hear from.

## Page structure

1. Header
2. Three direct paths
3. What to include (so the first message is useful)
4. Response expectation
5. Closing portrait + signature

---

## SECTION — HEADER

**Eyebrow:** `CONTACT`

**Display title (display-l):**
> *The fastest way is direct.*

**Lede (body-l, max 60ch):**
> Three paths below — pick whichever fits the conversation you want to have. I read every message myself.

---

## SECTION — THREE PATHS

A 3-column block on desktop, stacked on mobile. Each column has: a small icon (Phosphor regular, 24px), a label (--t-meta), a primary line (--t-headline), and a one-line description (body-s).

### Path 1 — Email

`DIRECT EMAIL`

**yoann@yoannleny.com**

For introductions, project briefs, and longer messages. Best for senior conversations.

### Path 2 — LinkedIn

`LINKEDIN`

**@yoannleny**

For peers, recruiters, and networking. Lower priority than email.

### Path 3 — Calendar

`30-MINUTE CALL`

**Book directly**

A pre-screened slot for serious project conversations. Use the email path first if it's a longer brief.

---

## SECTION — WHAT TO INCLUDE

A small editorial block — useful, slightly self-aware, helps the right people self-qualify.

**Eyebrow:** `IF WE'RE LIKELY TO WORK TOGETHER`

**Body (body, max 60ch):**
> A useful first message usually contains four things:
>
> 1. Where the company is today — stage, size, sector.
> 2. What you've already tried.
> 3. The decision you're trying to make, not the symptom.
> 4. The horizon — when does this need to be working.
>
> Three sentences each is more than enough. Most of the conversation happens after we talk.

---

## SECTION — RESPONSE EXPECTATION

A single line, body-s, --ink-500, italic.

> *I read messages within 24 hours, Monday to Friday. I respond to most within 48. If I can't help, I'll usually say so quickly and try to point you toward someone who can.*
