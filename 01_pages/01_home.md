## Page-level technical notes

- One canonical H1 per page — the hero headline.
- Each section is a `<section>` with an `aria-labelledby` pointing to its eyebrow heading (visually styled as eyebrow but semantically `<h2>`).
- Skip-to-content link appears at the very top of the DOM.
- The metric numbers count up on first viewport entry — 800ms duration, easeOutCubic. Skip the count-up entirely under reduced motion; just render the final value.
- The capability rows are real `<button>` elements with `aria-expanded` for the preview.
- The testimonial crossfade respects `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` by switching to a static stack of all quotes.
