# 01 — Brand Positioning

## The one-line positioning

> **Yoann Leny builds AI-enabled operating systems for modern organizations.**

That sentence is the north star. Every page, every section, every paragraph either earns it or removes it.

## Strategic positioning

Yoann sits at a rare intersection: he is fluent enough in **engineering** to architect a medallion lakehouse, fluent enough in **AI** to design multi-agent systems, and senior enough in **organizational design** to scale 40+ experts across three regions. Most people who can do any one of these cannot do the other two.

The brand must communicate this triangle without ever listing it as bullet points. The triangle is shown through the work, not stated.

## The four positioning pillars

### 1. Operator, not consultant
Yoann ships and runs systems in production. He is not a strategist who hands a deck and leaves. The site should never use the word *consulting*. Instead use: *building*, *running*, *scaling*, *architecting*.

### 2. Systems, not tools
Tools are commodities. Operating systems are leverage. Every concept page reinforces that the value is in how components compose into a system — not in which component you chose.

### 3. Margin, not vanity
Data and AI must produce business outcomes. Every metric on the site is a margin metric: revenue impact, utilization uplift, decision speed. No vanity numbers (lines of code, models trained, queries run).

### 4. Restraint, not theater
The aesthetic itself is the proof. A noisy, animated, AI-buzzword-heavy site would contradict the message. Restraint = signal of seniority.

## Audience targets (in priority order)

1. **CTOs and Chief Data Officers** at companies between Series C and mid-cap who need a senior operator to set up or rescue their data and AI function.
2. **CEOs and COOs** who feel they are paying too much for too little data and AI output and don't know who to trust.
3. **Boards and PE operating partners** evaluating AI readiness of portfolio companies.
4. **Senior peers** (other VPs of Data, AI architects) — for credibility, referrals, and recruiting.
5. **Recruiters and headhunters** for VP/SVP/Chief-level roles.

The site is *not* targeted at junior engineers, generic recruiters, or vendors trying to sell.

## Voice and tone

| Trait | Yes | No |
|---|---|---|
| Register | Executive, calm, precise | Casual, hype, breezy |
| Sentence length | Short. Then longer when an idea genuinely needs it. | Long flowing run-ons |
| Person | First-person singular ("I") | Royal "we", third-person, brand-speak |
| Verbs | Build, run, ship, scale, architect, orchestrate, decide | Empower, unlock, leverage, transform, revolutionize |
| Numbers | Specific ($13M, 40+ experts, 15%) | Vague ("significant impact", "many people") |
| Buzzwords | Used sparingly, always defined | Stacked without explanation |
| Confidence | Stated as fact, not opinion | Hedged ("I think maybe") |

## Words to use

architect, build, ship, scale, operate, orchestrate, system, platform, layer, contract, lineage, governance, semantic, agentic, autonomous, deterministic, observable, evaluable, margin, leverage, decision, throughput, utilization, executive, principal, operator.

## Words to avoid

empower, unlock, leverage (verb), revolutionize, transform, journey, ecosystem, synergy, holistic, world-class, best-in-class, cutting-edge, next-gen, AI-first, AI-powered (without specifics), thought leader, ninja, rockstar, guru, hustle, grind.

## Trust signals to feature

- Concrete revenue numbers ($13M+)
- Specific scope (3 regions, 40+ experts)
- Named technologies in the stack (without making them the hero)
- Editorial writing depth (the concept library)
- Clean photography
- A direct executive contact path (not a form)

## Anti-signals to avoid

- Generic stock photos of glowing brains or blue grids
- Lottie animations of robots
- Logos of "as featured in" press unless genuinely substantial
- Sliding testimonial walls with seven quotes
- Newsletter pop-ups
- Cookie banners that are louder than the content
- Anything that pulses, bounces, or autoplays
